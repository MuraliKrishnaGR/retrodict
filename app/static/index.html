Retrodict

What most likely happened before this photo was taken.

Choose or take a photo of a table, a counter or a desk. Retrodict finds the objects, reads the traces they carry (steam, crumbs, a torn wrapper, a lowered drink) and works backward to what happened, with a time range for each step and the evidence behind it.
